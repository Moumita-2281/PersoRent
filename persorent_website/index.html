Trust and safety

Important checks before launch

These are must-have rules for a real marketplace in India.

Legal vehicle usage

Do not allow illegal private-vehicle renting. Check RC, permit, insurance, PUC and local rules before approving any listing.

Human review

Use bot verification for speed, but keep admin review for risky accounts, fake documents, disputes and high-value cars.

Damage dispute rules

Require timestamped photos, odometer image, fuel image, pickup OTP, return OTP, and clear claim deadline.

Secure payments

Hold rent and deposits separately. Release owner payout only after return confirmation or dispute resolution.
